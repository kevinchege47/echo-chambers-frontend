Why it matters

Information doesn't spread equally. It mutates.

Narratives evolve as they move through communities, platforms, and recommendation systems. A single claim can become distorted, amplified, repeated, and eventually accepted as truth simply through repetition.

Echo Chambers exists to expose how information changes over time. Instead of looking only at whether something is true or false, we focus on how narratives travel, who amplifies them, and how communities reinforce them.

The Pipeline

Information rarely travels directly. It passes through interpreters.

Echo Chambers simulates how a neutral fact gradually changes as different actors rewrite, summarize, exaggerate, speculate and reinterpret it. Each agent introduces its own biases and incentives.

📰

Wire Service

Compresses stories for brevity and removes context, uncertainty and nuance.

🔥

Tabloid Blog

Converts information into emotionally charged, click-driven headlines.

📱

Social Influencer

Turns information into personal experiences and shareable content.

🎙️

Podcast Host

Connects unrelated ideas and introduces speculation as explanation.

📺

TV News Anchor

Prioritizes urgency and dramatic interpretation over scientific nuance.

💬

Random Internet User

Retells information from memory, mixing facts with opinion.

🏛️

Government Official

Frames information through policy and institutional messaging.

🎓

Academic Commenter

Adds complexity, jargon and personal theories to the discussion.

Original Fact → Super Agent selects path → Multiple agent rewrites → Distortion score calculated → Final narrative
